1. ABOUT US (TENTANG KAMI)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PT. CHANTIKA BAHARI SEJAHTERA ABADI has grown to become one of the largest 
independent port & shipping services provider in standards region. Based in 
Batam, Indonesia.

We guarantee a fast and dependable service to all our customers. In our 
endeavours to perfection.

PT. CHANTIKA BAHARI SEJAHTERA ABADI provide a comprehensive range of shipping 
service, agency and logistics services to the maritime industry. With a team of 
dedicated and experienced professionals that are well versed with handling all 
port & regulatory formalities for all types of vessel that call on Batam Port 
and supporting vessel with all required provisions, spare parts & other 
services, customer can be assured of a fuss free and swift port call under our 
agency.

PT. CHANTIKA BAHARI SEJAHTERA ABADI Takes it's responsibilities in respect of 
Health, Safety and the Environment (HSE) to the heart of all it's activities. 
It upholds the highest international standards of HSE, financial integrity and 
corporate ethics.
